## 📲 Overview of Connected Apps

Aradhya ERP isn't just a web dashboard—it is a connected mobile ecosystem ensuring all school stakeholders stay informed anywhere, anytime:

### 1. 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Mobile App (Android & iOS)
- **Instant Attendance Alerts:** Push notification the moment the student enters or leaves the school.
- **Online Fee Payment:** View pending fee slips and pay directly via UPI, NetBanking, or Debit Card with instant PDF receipts.
- **Homework & Notices:** Digital diary, daily homework assignments, and teacher voice notes.
- **Report Cards:** Semester marks, grading breakdown, remarks, and downloadable digital report cards.
- **Live Bus Tracking:** Real-time GPS location of the school bus with arrival estimate.

### 2. 👩‍🏫 Teacher & Staff App (Android & iOS)
- **One-Tap Attendance:** Mark class attendance in less than 30 seconds.
- **Homework Assignment:** Upload homework with attachments and photos directly from the classroom.
- **Marks & Grading:** Enter exam marks on-the-go with automatic total and percentage calculations.
- **Staff Leave Application:** Apply for leaves and track balance without paperwork.

### 3. 🚌 Driver & Bus Assistant App (Android)
- **GPS Broadcasting:** Continuous telemetry broadcasting to parents and transport managers.
- **Pickup / Drop Checklist:** Verify students boarding each bus stop with single tap.
- **Emergency SOS:** Instant emergency broadcast to school management.
