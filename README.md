## 🌟 Detailed Feature Breakdown

### 1. 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Mobile App
- **Live Attendance Feed:** Instant notifications when your child is marked present or absent.
- **Direct UPI Fee Payment:** View upcoming fee dues and pay online with instant digital receipts.
- **Digital Diary & Homework:** Receive class assignments, teacher remarks, and circular notices.
- **Report Cards:** View semester exam marks, grade breakdowns, and download official report cards.
- **GPS Bus Tracking:** Track the live school bus location on map with arrival ETA alerts.

### 2. 👩‍🏫 Teacher & Staff Mobile App
- **One-Tap Attendance:** Mark class attendance in less than 30 seconds from your phone.
- **Homework Assignment:** Upload homework with attachments and photos directly from the classroom.
- **Marks & Grading Entry:** Enter exam marks on-the-go with automatic total and percentage calculations.
- **Staff Leave Application:** Apply for leaves and track balance without paperwork.

### 3. 🚌 Driver & Bus Assistant App
- **Live GPS Broadcasting:** Continuous route telemetry broadcasting to parents and transport managers.
- **Pickup / Drop Checklist:** Verify students boarding each bus stop with single tap.
- **Emergency SOS:** Instant emergency broadcast to school management.
